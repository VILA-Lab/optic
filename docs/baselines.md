# Methods and fitting settings

The main comparison evaluates nine methods at approximately 100× PLE compression.
[fit_settings.json](../configs/paper/fit_settings.json) records the settings for each
checkpoint and [paper_tables.json](../configs/paper/paper_tables.json) maps them to
the paper. Run scripts from the repository root.

| Method | Builder | Configuration and settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PQ | `builders/baselines/baseline_packed_pq.py` | The nominal 100x setting uses 4 subspaces, 1,024 centroids (10-bit codes) and 8 k-means iterations; the per-setting values are in `configs/paper/fit_settings.json`. E2B BF16 and W4A16 cells are driven by `scripts/build_baselines.py build-command` with `configs/fit/baselines_e2b_bf16.json` or `configs/fit/baselines_w4a16.json`. |
| RVQ | `builders/baselines/baseline_residual_vq.py` | Greedy residual vector quantization; the E4B BF16 100x setting uses 4 codebooks of 1,024 centroids and 8 iterations, and the checkpoint-specific settings are in `fit_settings.json` and the two matrix configurations. |
| OPQ-P | `scripts/build_opq.py` | Parametric optimized product quantization: PCA eigenvectors are assigned to equal-width subspaces by greedy determinant balancing, without token exceptions. At 100x it uses 4 subspaces, 1,024 centroids and 8 iterations; the checkpoint-specific settings are in `fit_settings.json` and the matrix configurations. |
| AQ | `scripts/build_aq.py`, then `scripts/finalize_aq.py` | 10 outer sweeps, fitting beam 16, final encoding beam 64 (`configs/fit/aq_bf16.json`, `configs/fit/aq_w4a16.json`). |
| AQLM Euclidean beam encoder | `scripts/build_aqlm_e4b.py` (E4B BF16 100x and E2B BF16 via `scripts/build_aqlm_e2b.py`), `scripts/build_aqlm_w4a16.py` (W4A16) | Uses the AQLM author beam search on the Euclidean objective; `configs/fit/aqlm_w4a16.json` and `fit_settings.json` give codebooks, code width and beam size. |
| INT8 randomized low-rank SVD | `builders/baselines/baseline_quant_lowrank_factor.py` | The E4B BF16 100x setting uses rank 207, 16 oversampling columns and 4 power iterations with INT8 factors and float16 scales; the other settings are in `fit_settings.json`. |
| Output-path-weighted PCA-PQ | Source build `builders/methods/dout_weighted_pca_rotated_pq_scaled_exceptions.py`, then wrapper `builders/methods/affine_calibrated_pca_rotated_pq_scaled_exceptions.py` | At 100x: 4 subspaces, 1,024 centroids, 8 iterations, 128 exception rows at scale 0.5 selected by calibration-text frequency, tempering exponent alpha_w = 0.375, sensitivity floor epsilon_w = 0.02, then a scale-only layer-coordinate calibration with alpha 0.5 and eps 1e-6. Drivers: `scripts/build_weighted_pca_pq_e2b.py` (config `configs/fit/weighted_pca_pq_e2b_bf16.json`) and `scripts/build_weighted_pca_pq_qat.py` (configs `configs/fit/weighted_pca_pq_e2b_w4a16.json` or `configs/fit/weighted_pca_pq_e4b_w4a16.json`). Coordinate weights: `scripts/derive_pca_pq_weights_e2b.py` (config `configs/calibration/pca_pq_weights_e2b_qat.json`) computes mean squared gate times squared projection column norm, normalizes to mean one, floors at 0.02 and renormalizes. |
| OPTIC | E4B BF16: `scripts/build_optic_e4b_bf16.py` with `configs/fit/optic_e4b_bf16.json` (100x). Other settings: `scripts/build_optic.py --config <config> --device cuda:0` with `configs/fit/optic_e2b_bf16.json`, `configs/fit/optic_e2b_w4a16.json` or `configs/fit/optic_e4b_w4a16.json`. All call `builders/methods/optic.py`. | E4B at 100x: latent width 644, 161 groups of width 4, 256 codewords per group; E2B at 100x: latent width 560, 140 groups. Eigenvalue-balanced groups, output-path-weighted PCA initialization, 25 k-means iterations, AdamW with learning rate 3e-4, zero weight decay, batch 8,192, gradient clipping at norm 1, 10 alternations of 500 updates (5,000 updates) with 2 conditional reassignment sweeps after each block through update 4,500, unused-code repair (minimum usage 1), assignment ridge 1e-8 times the mean diagonal of the latent metric (the scale-relative latent-norm regularizer), metric eigenvalue floor 1e-6, seed 0. The group counts of the other settings are in their configurations. |
| Frequency-tiered OPTIC | E4B BF16 100x: `scripts/fit_tiered_optic_e4b.py` (recipe `configs/fit/tiered_optic_e4b_bf16_recipe.json`), then `scripts/finalize_tiered_optic_e4b.py --fit-dir <fit-dir> --output-dir artifacts/frequency-adaptive-variable-rate-cross-layer-latent-vq/100x` and `python -m ple_eval.materialize`. W4A16 checkpoints: `scripts/build_tiered_optic.py run --build-config <config> --device cuda:0` with `configs/fit/tiered_optic_e2b_w4a16.json` or `configs/fit/tiered_optic_e4b_w4a16.json`; E2B BF16: `scripts/build_tiered_optic_e2b.py run`. | E4B BF16 100x: M0 = 160 base groups, M1 = 200 groups, group width 4, 256 codewords, N_freq = 110,411 most frequent non-special tokens, N_sp = 24 special-token IDs kept exactly in BF16, INT8 decoder weights with float16 scales and biases, 56,371,428 resident bytes. The other recipes record their own M0, M1 and N_freq. |
